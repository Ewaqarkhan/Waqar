Publications

My published and under-review work spans AI-driven intrusion detection, explainable AI for security, federated learning for smart grids, and deep learning for medical diagnosis. Below is the complete list of peer-reviewed publications, organized by status. Full text and preprints are available on my Google Scholar profile.

Khan, W., et al. · Internet of Things (Elsevier, IF: 7.1) · Under Review

A framework that combines model quantization with explainability techniques to deploy accurate intrusion-detection models on resource-constrained IoT devices, addressing the trade-off between detection accuracy and on-device inference cost.

Few-Shot Learning for EEG-Based Diagnosis of Alzheimer's and Frontotemporal Dementia

Khan, W. · Under Review

Proposes a few-shot learning approach to diagnose neurodegenerative diseases from EEG signals, tackling the data-scarcity problem that limits clinical-deployment of deep diagnostic models.

Published and Accepted

Federated Learning-Based NIDS for Smart Grid: The Impact of Feature Selection and Model Size

Khan, W., et al. · IEEE AIoT Conference, 2026 · Accepted

Investigates how feature-selection strategies and model-size constraints interact in federated network intrusion detection systems deployed across distributed smart-grid environments, where data cannot be centralized due to privacy and bandwidth limits.

A Novel Transformer-Based Explainable AI Approach Using SHAP for Intrusion Detection in VANETs

Khan, W., et al. · Computer Networks (Elsevier, IF: 4.6), 2025 · Published

Introduces a transformer architecture for intrusion detection in Vehicular Ad-hoc Networks (VANETs) and couples it with SHAP-based explainability so that human analysts can interpret which traffic features drove each alert — a key requirement for trustworthy deployment in safety-critical transportation systems.

Enhancing Security in 6G-Enabled Wireless Sensor Networks for Smart Cities

Khan, W., et al. · Frontiers in Sustainable Cities, 2025 · Published

Presents a multi-deep-learning intrusion detection approach for 6G-enabled wireless sensor networks supporting smart-city infrastructure, analyzing time-series data flows to flag anomalous behaviour that conventional rule-based systems fail to capture.

Explainable and Optimized Hybrid Deep Learning Model for Early Diagnosis of Alzheimer's and Frontotemporal Dementia

Khan, W., et al. · Frontiers in Medicine, 2025 · Published

Develops a hybrid deep-learning model optimized for early diagnosis of Alzheimer's and Frontotemporal dementia, paired with explainability to support clinician trust in model predictions during the critical early-screening stage.

Research Interests

My research agenda is anchored on making AI-driven security systems both accurate and trustworthy. Concretely, I work on four interlocking threads:

  • Temporal anomaly detection in continuous network-traffic streams using VAE-LSTM, Transformers, and Temporal Convolutional Networks, with explicit handling of concept drift and missing-signal robustness.
  • Explainable AI for security — turning opaque deep-model decisions into human-readable cause attribution so SOC analysts and XDR pipelines can act on alerts with confidence.
  • Lightweight / TinyML deployment — quantization and architecture search to fit capable detectors onto IoT hardware with tight memory and energy budgets.
  • Federated & split learning — privacy-preserving training under non-IID data and network degradation, with a focus on smart-grid and VANET environments.
